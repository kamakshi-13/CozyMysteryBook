A NOTE BEFORE YOU BEGIN

How to play
the little mystery book.

Take your time. Make notes. Enjoy the clues. There is no timer inside a puzzle, only a fresh case waiting each day.

01

Word search · Find the motive

Tap letters in order to make each hidden word. Words may travel in any direction. Find them all to uncover the motive clue.

🔎
02

Mini Sudoku · Find the room

Fill every row, column and 2×3 box with numbers 1 to 6. Use pencil mode for notes. Undo and Reset are there whenever you need them. The golden square gives you the room code: 1–2 Kitchen, 3 Library, 4–5 Conservatory, 6 Cellar.

▦
03

Number pattern · Find the time

Look at the gaps between the numbers. Work out the rule, enter the next number, and reveal when the incident happened.

✳
04

Murdoku · Find the killer

Place five people on a five-by-five floor plan. Each person appears once, and each row and column holds one person. Use pencil notes to track possibilities. Placed people cross out the rest of their row and column. Undo and Reset are available.

♟
05

Hidden objects · Find the weapon

Search the garden for three tiny objects. When you find them all, a final piece of evidence appears. Tap it to reveal the weapon clue.

🌿
06

Make your accusation · Read the story

Use the clue board to choose the killer, motive, location and weapon. A correct accusation closes the case and unlocks the complete short story.
